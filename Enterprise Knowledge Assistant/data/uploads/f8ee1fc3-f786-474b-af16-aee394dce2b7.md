# Learning and Development Policy

## 1. Learning Budget

### 1.1 Annual Budget
Every employee receives an individual learning and development budget of ₹40,000 per financial year. The budget covers courses, books, conferences, and workshops. Unused budget does not carry forward to the next financial year.

### 1.2 Budget Approval
Expenses against the learning budget require manager approval before purchase. Reimbursement is processed through the standard expense system with receipts attached, within 30 days of purchase.

## 2. Certifications

### 2.1 Reimbursable Certifications
The company reimburses the full cost of role-relevant professional certifications up to ₹75,000 per year. Eligible examples include cloud certifications, project management certifications, and product-related certifications listed in the L&D catalogue.

### 2.2 Service Obligation
Employees who claim certification reimbursement agree to remain with the company for at least 12 months after the certification date. If the employee leaves before 12 months, the reimbursement is recovered on a pro-rated basis from the final settlement.

### 2.3 Exam Leave
Employees are entitled to 2 paid days of exam leave per certification attempt, in addition to annual leave. Exam leave requires manager approval at least 2 weeks in advance and proof of exam registration.

## 3. Training Days and Mandatory Compliance

### 3.1 Training Days
Employees are entitled to 3 paid training days per year for structured courses, bootcamps, or internal workshops. Training days cannot be combined with annual leave and must be used in the financial year they are allocated.

### 3.2 Mandatory Compliance Training
The following compliance trainings are mandatory for all employees and must be completed annually: information security awareness, prevention of sexual harassment (POSH), and the ethics and code of conduct course. Non-completion by the deadline results in escalation to the department head and a block on further reimbursements.

## 4. Internal Academy

### 4.1 Course Catalogue
The internal Academy offers over 20 self-paced courses covering leadership, communication, data skills, and product knowledge. Courses are available on the learning portal and count toward the individual learning budget at zero cost.

### 4.2 Internal Trainers
Employees who deliver Academy sessions or mentor new joiners for more than 20 hours in a quarter are eligible for a recognition bonus of ₹10,000 per quarter, subject to manager nomination.
