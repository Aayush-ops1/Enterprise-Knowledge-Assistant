# New Employee Onboarding Guide

## 1. Before Your First Day

### 1.1 Documents Required
New joiners must submit their PAN card, Aadhaar, proof of address, and latest educational certificates to HR at least 5 working days before the joining date. The background check is a conditional requirement for joining and is conducted by the third-party vendor engaged by the HR team.

### 1.2 Pre-joining Communication
The onboarding coordinator sends a welcome email 3 working days before the joining date with the offer letter, the joining checklist, and the date, time, and venue for day-one induction.

## 2. Day Zero — IT and Access Setup

### 2.1 Equipment
Every new employee receives a company laptop, a charger, and a backpack on day zero. The laptop is pre-configured with full-disk encryption and endpoint protection before it is handed over.

### 2.2 Accounts Provisioned
The following accounts are created within 24 hours of joining: company email, Slack, Jira, the HR portal, and VPN access. Multi-factor authentication must be enrolled on the first login using the company MDM enrollment link.

### 2.3 Access Requests
Additional system access beyond the default set must be requested through the IT helpdesk portal with manager approval. Access requests are typically fulfilled within 2 working days.

## 3. First Week

### 3.1 Induction Schedule
The first day includes an HR induction covering company policies, payroll, and benefits. A security awareness session is mandatory and must be completed within 7 days of joining; the system revokes network access if the training is not completed by the deadline.

### 3.2 Buddy Programme
Each new joiner is assigned a buddy from their team who is responsible for answering day-to-day questions, explaining team rituals, and introducing the new joiner to key stakeholders during the first month.

### 3.3 Tool Training
Team-specific tool training and codebase walkthroughs are scheduled in the first two weeks by the hiring manager. New joiners are expected to have a local development environment running by the end of week two.

## 4. 30-60-90 Day Plan

### 4.1 First 30 Days
The focus is on learning: completing all mandatory trainings, meeting every team member, and shadowing at least one customer support session for customer-facing roles.

### 4.2 Days 31-60
The focus shifts to contributing: the new joiner takes ownership of at least one small task or bug fix, presents a learnings review to the team, and completes the first performance check-in with the manager.

### 4.3 Days 61-90
The new joiner is expected to take on a full ownership item from the team backlog and complete the probation review with the manager and HR.

## 5. Probation

### 5.1 Duration and Review
Probation lasts 3 months for all roles. The probation review evaluates performance against the 90-day plan, adherence to company values, and attendance. Probation may be extended once by up to 3 additional months if the review is not conclusive.

### 5.2 Confirmation
On successful completion of probation, the employee receives a confirmation letter and becomes eligible for benefits that are restricted during probation, including work-from-home days and the home-office setup allowance.
