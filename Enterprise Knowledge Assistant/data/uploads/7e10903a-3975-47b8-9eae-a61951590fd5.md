# Recruitment and Hiring Policy

## 1. Hiring Requisitions

### 1.1 Requisition Process
All hiring begins with a requisition submitted by the hiring manager through the HR portal. The requisition must include the job description, the proposed level, and the hiring justification. Approvals are required from the department head and the HR Business Partner before the role is opened.

### 1.2 Approval SLAs
Requisition approval for backfills and new roles at levels E1 to E3 takes up to 5 working days. Approvals at level E4 and above additionally require the CTO or CHRO sign-off and take up to 10 working days.

## 2. Interview Process

### 2.1 Interview Loop
The standard interview loop consists of a screening call, followed by two technical or functional rounds, and a final culture-fit round with the hiring manager. The maximum number of interview rounds is five, and candidates are never required to complete more than three onsite rounds in a single visit.

### 2.2 Evaluation and Feedback
Every interviewer must submit structured feedback within 48 hours of the interview. Hiring decisions are made in a debrief session led by the HR Business Partner, and offers require approval from both the hiring manager and the HR Business Partner.

### 2.3 Offer Levels
Offers are made within the salary band for the level (see the compensation policy). A verbal offer expires after 5 working days, and the written offer letter is issued within 3 working days of verbal acceptance.

## 3. Employee Referrals

### 3.1 Referral Bonus
Employees can refer candidates through the referral portal. The referral bonus is ₹25,000 for mid-level hires (E2-E3) and ₹50,000 for senior hires (E4 and above). The bonus is paid in the payroll cycle following the completion of the referred candidate's 6-month anniversary.

### 3.2 Referral Eligibility
Referrals from employees in the HR team, hiring panel members, and direct reports of the hiring manager are not eligible for the bonus. Referrals for the same candidate by multiple employees are attributed to the first referral received.

## 4. Background Checks

### 4.1 Scope of Checks
Background checks cover education verification, employment history with the last two employers, and criminal record screening. Checks are conducted by an approved third-party vendor.

### 4.2 Conditional Offers
All offers are conditional on a satisfactory background check. If an adverse finding is received, HR will share the report with the candidate and give them 5 working days to respond before a final decision is made. Any offer made against an outstanding background check is revoked if the check is not cleared before the joining date.

## 5. Probation and Confirmation

### 5.1 Probation Period
All new hires serve a probation period of 3 months. Probation may be extended once by up to 3 months with written notice from HR.

### 5.2 Early Confirmation
Exceptional performers may be confirmed early after 2 months of probation, on the recommendation of the hiring manager and approval from the department head and HR Business Partner.
