# Office Policies and Facilities Guide

## 1. Working Hours

### 1.1 Core Hours
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST, Monday to Friday. Employees may flex their start and end times by up to 2 hours with manager approval, as long as core collaboration hours between 11:00 AM and 4:00 PM are covered.

### 1.2 Compressed Weeks
Employees in eligible roles may request a 9-day fortnight (alternate Fridays off) after completing 6 months of service. The request requires manager and HR approval and is reviewed annually.

## 2. Holidays

### 2.1 National Holidays
The company observes 14 national holidays per calendar year, including Republic Day, Independence Day, Gandhi Jayanti, and Diwali.

### 2.2 Floating Holidays
Each employee receives 3 floating holidays per year that can be taken on any working day with prior manager approval. Floating holidays must be used within the calendar year and cannot be carried forward.

## 3. Security and Access

### 3.1 ID Badges
Every employee must wear their ID badge at all times while inside any Acme office. Lost badges must be reported to the facilities team immediately; a replacement badge costs ₹500.

### 3.2 Visitors
Visitors must sign in at the reception desk, be issued a visitor badge, and be escorted by the host employee at all times within the office. Visitor access is logged and retained for audit purposes.

### 3.3 After-Hours Access
Access to the office after 9:00 PM requires the employee to be registered in the after-hours access list by their manager. Security guards perform a floor check before closing the building at 11:00 PM.

## 4. Facilities

### 4.1 Meeting Rooms
Meeting rooms are booked through the Outlook room booking system. Rooms can be booked for up to 2 hours at a time; bookings longer than 2 hours require admin assistance. Unused rooms are automatically released after 15 minutes.

### 4.2 Pantry and Cafeteria
Pantries are located on the 2nd and 4th floors of the Bengaluru office, stocked with coffee, tea, and snacks. Lunch is subsidised at ₹50 per meal in the cafeteria, available from 12:30 PM to 2:30 PM.

### 4.3 Parking
Two-wheeler parking is free for all employees. Car parking is available at ₹500 per month, allocated on a first-come-first-served basis through the facilities portal.

## 5. Housekeeping Rules

### 5.1 Desk Cleaning
Employees are expected to keep their workstations clean and secure. Laptops must be locked whenever the employee is away from the desk, and confidential documents must not be left unattended on desks overnight.

### 5.2 Personal Deliveries
Personal courier deliveries are permitted at the reception desk only between 10:00 AM and 6:00 PM. The company is not responsible for the loss of personal items.
