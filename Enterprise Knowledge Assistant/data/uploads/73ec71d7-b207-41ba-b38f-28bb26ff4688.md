# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. General Rules

### 1.1 Eligible Expenses
Employees may claim business expenses that are incurred for the company and supported by valid invoices or receipts. Personal expenses are never reimbursable, and mixing personal items on company invoices is prohibited.

### 1.2 Submission Deadline
Expenses must be submitted through the expense system within 60 days of the date of spend. Expenses submitted after 60 days require the finance controller's approval for exception. Reimbursements are processed within 15 working days of a complete and approved submission, and are credited to the employee's bank account.

### 1.3 Receipt Requirements
All expenses above ₹500 require an itemised receipt. GST-compliant e-invoices are preferred. Meal allowances up to the per-diem caps do not require itemised receipts.

## 2. Approval Thresholds

### 2.1 Approval Matrix
- Expenses up to ₹5,000: manager approval only.
- Expenses from ₹5,000 to ₹50,000: manager and department head approval.
- Expenses above ₹50,000: manager, department head, and finance controller approval before reimbursement.

### 2.2 Advance Requests
Employees travelling or making large purchases may request a cash advance of up to ₹1 lakh through the expense system. Advances must be settled with receipts within 30 days of return or purchase, and a new advance is not released until previous advances are settled.

## 3. Local Conveyance and Meals

### 3.1 Conveyance
Local travel for business is reimbursed at ₹12 per kilometre for personal vehicles, or at actual metered fare for taxis and auto-rickshaws booked through approved aggregators. Fuel claims require a log of the trips with dates and purpose.

### 3.2 Meal Allowance
The per-diem meal allowance is ₹1,200 per day for domestic travel and USD 60 per day for international travel. Claims within these caps do not need itemised receipts.

## 4. Corporate Cards

### 4.1 Eligibility
Corporate cards are issued to managers and above, and to employees whose role requires frequent client travel, on approval from the finance controller. Card limits are set per level and can be revised by finance.

### 4.2 Card Usage Rules
Corporate cards must be used only for business expenses. Personal spend on a corporate card is a disciplinary matter, and the employee is required to reimburse the amount immediately with an explanation. Card statements must be reconciled within 15 days of month end.

## 5. Exceptions and Audits

### 5.1 Expense Audits
The finance team conducts a quarterly audit of a random sample of at least 10% of expense claims. Claims found to be personal or overstated are recovered from the employee's salary and flagged to HR for disciplinary review.

### 5.2 Policy Exceptions
Any deviation from this policy requires written approval from the finance controller before the expense is incurred. Retrospective exceptions are granted only in genuine emergency situations and are documented by finance.
