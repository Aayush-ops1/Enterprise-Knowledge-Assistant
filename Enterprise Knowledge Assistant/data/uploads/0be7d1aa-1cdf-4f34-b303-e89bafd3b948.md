# Business Travel Policy

## 1. Booking Rules

### 1.1 Booking Channel
All business travel must be booked through the approved travel portal to qualify for company rates and travel insurance. Booking outside the portal requires prior approval from the department head.

### 1.2 Advance Booking
Bookings made 14 or more days in advance use the best available fare class. Bookings made less than 7 days before travel are classified as last-minute and require manager justification in the travel request.

## 2. Air Travel

### 2.1 Airfare Class by Duration
- Flights under 4 hours: Economy class.
- Domestic flights over 4 hours: Economy class, with Business class allowed for Director level and above.
- International flights under 6 hours: Economy class.
- International flights between 6 and 10 hours: Premium Economy.
- International flights over 10 hours: Business class for all travellers.

### 2.2 Exceptions
Upgrades to business class may be approved by the department head for medical reasons or when the traveller carries confidential materials requiring secure handling. Frequent-flyer miles earned on company travel belong to the company and are pooled for future company bookings.

## 3. Hotels

### 3.1 Hotel Caps
Hotel stays are capped at ₹6,000 per night in metro cities and ₹4,000 per night in other Indian cities. International stays are capped at USD 180 per night. Caps exclude breakfast and taxes. Stays above the cap require department head approval before booking.

### 3.2 Extended Stays
Stays longer than 14 consecutive nights require approval from the finance controller and are reviewed for alternative accommodation arrangements.

## 4. Per Diem and Incidentals

### 4.1 Per Diem Rates
The per-diem allowance is ₹1,500 per day for domestic travel and USD 65 per day for international travel. Half the per-diem rate applies on travel days (departure and return days).

### 4.2 Incidentals
Laundry, local calls, and internet are covered by the per-diem and do not require separate claims.

## 5. Travel Safety

### 5.1 Travel Insurance
Travel insurance is mandatory for all international business travel and is automatically included when bookings are made through the travel portal. Domestic travel is covered under the company's group accident policy.

### 5.2 Emergency Assistance
Travellers should save the 24x7 travel assistance helpline number in their phones. Any travel disruption or safety incident must be reported to the facilities and security team within 4 hours of the event.
