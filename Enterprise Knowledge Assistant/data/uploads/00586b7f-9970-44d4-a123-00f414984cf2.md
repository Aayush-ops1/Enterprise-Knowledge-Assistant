# Acme Corporation — Company Profile

## 1. About the Company

### 1.1 Overview
Acme Corporation is a software products company founded in 2015 and headquartered in Bengaluru, India. The company builds enterprise SaaS products for mid-market and enterprise customers, with a current headcount of approximately 450 employees across engineering, sales, marketing, finance, and operations.

### 1.2 Mission and Values
Our mission is to make business operations simple for growing companies. The company operates on four core values: Customer First, Own It, Ship It, and Stay Curious. All hiring, promotions, and performance reviews are evaluated against these values.

## 2. Product Lines

### 2.1 Acme ERP
Acme ERP is the flagship product, an integrated suite covering procurement, inventory, finance, and HR modules for mid-market manufacturers and distributors. It accounts for approximately 60% of company revenue.

### 2.2 AcmePay
AcmePay is a payments and invoicing product launched in 2022 that handles invoicing, payment collections, and reconciliation for small businesses. It accounts for approximately 25% of revenue.

### 2.3 AcmeInsights
AcmeInsights is the analytics and reporting layer, launched in 2024, providing dashboards and forecasting on top of Acme ERP data. It accounts for the remaining 15% of revenue.

## 3. Organisation Structure

### 3.1 Leadership Team
The company is led by a Chief Executive Officer (CEO). Direct reports are the Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO), Chief Financial Officer (CFO), Chief Technology Officer (CTO), Chief Marketing Officer (CMO), and the Vice President of Operations.

### 3.2 Departments
The company is organised into six departments: HR, Finance, Legal, Engineering, Operations, and Sales & Marketing. Engineering is the largest department with approximately 180 employees, followed by Sales & Marketing with 120.

## 4. Offices

### 4.1 Locations
Acme has four offices: the headquarters in Bengaluru, and smaller offices in Mumbai, Pune, and Hyderabad. A fifth office in Chennai is planned to open in 2026.

### 4.2 Working Model
Employees at the Bengaluru headquarters work a hybrid model with three days in office per week. The Mumbai, Pune, and Hyderabad offices operate a remote-first model with an optional desk booking system.

## 5. Financial Snapshot

### 5.1 Revenue
Acme Corporation closed FY 2025 with revenue of approximately ₹480 crore. The company has been profitable for six consecutive quarters and reinvests roughly 18% of revenue into research and development.

### 5.2 Customer Base
The company serves over 1,200 paying customers across India, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East, with a customer retention rate of 94% year over year.
