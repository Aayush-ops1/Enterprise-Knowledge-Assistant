# IT Asset Management Policy

## 1. Standard Equipment

### 1.1 Laptop Standards
The standard issue laptop is the MacBook Pro 14-inch or the Dell Latitude 7440, based on the employee's role and team preference. Employees in engineering roles may choose either model; all other roles receive the Dell Latitude by default.

### 1.2 Refresh Cycle
Laptops are refreshed every 3 years from the date of issue. Devices older than 3 years are replaced through the asset refresh programme, which runs twice a year in April and October.

## 2. Asset Tracking

### 2.1 Asset Tags
Every asset is tagged with a unique asset ID and barcode at the time of purchase. Assets are registered in the asset management system with the owner's name, department, and location.

### 2.2 Annual Audit
A physical audit of all company assets is conducted every April. Employees must confirm the location and condition of their assigned assets within 5 working days of the audit request. Unverified assets are flagged to the department head.

## 3. Lost or Damaged Devices

### 3.1 Reporting
Loss or theft of any company device must be reported to the IT helpdesk and the security team within 24 hours of discovery. The report must include the asset ID, the last known location, and the circumstances.

### 3.2 Remote Wipe
On report of a lost or stolen device, the IT team remotely locks and wipes the device if it is enrolled in the device management platform. The employee must also report the loss to the police within 48 hours for devices containing Restricted data, and provide the FIR number to IT.

### 3.3 Cost of Damage
Accidental damage is covered by the company's device insurance. Loss due to negligence, such as leaving the device unattended in a public place, may result in the employee bearing up to 50% of the replacement cost, decided by the IT asset committee.

## 4. Returns and Disposal

### 4.1 Returns on Separation
Employees returning company assets must do so within 7 days of their last working day. The asset handover checklist includes the laptop, charger, backpack, and any peripheral devices issued.

### 4.2 Data Wiping and Disposal
Before disposal or reassignment, all data is wiped using a certified method compliant with NIST SP 800-88 standards. Disposal of decommissioned hardware is done through the approved e-waste vendor, and a disposal certificate is retained for audit.

## 5. Accessories and Peripherals

### 5.1 Accessory Budget
Employees are entitled to peripherals such as a monitor, keyboard, and mouse within a budget of ₹10,000 per 2-year period, ordered through the IT portal.

### 5.2 Software Assets
Software licences are tracked in the same asset system. Unused licences are reclaimed quarterly by the IT team, and employees may not install unapproved software on company devices without an IT ticket.
