# Vendor Onboarding and Offboarding Process

## 1. Vendor Onboarding Steps

### 1.1 Process Overview
Vendor onboarding follows five steps: request, due diligence, security review, contracting, and provisioning. Each step must be completed before the next begins, and the whole process is tracked in the vendor management portal.

### 1.2 Step Details
1. Request: the business owner submits a vendor request with the scope of work, expected value, and duration.
2. Due diligence: the procurement team verifies GST registration, financial stability, and two client references.
3. Security review: the Information Security team assesses the vendor's security posture.
4. Contracting: Legal executes the contract and any required data processing agreement.
5. Provisioning: access is created in company systems following least-privilege principles.

## 2. Timelines

### 2.1 Standard Timeline
The standard onboarding timeline is 15 working days from request submission to access provisioning. Timelines are extended to 25 working days when a data processing agreement is required or the vendor will access Restricted data.

### 2.2 Expedited Onboarding
Expedited onboarding in under 7 working days is available for low-risk vendors with a contract value under ₹5 lakh, at the discretion of the procurement lead.

## 3. Security Requirements

### 3.1 Security Questionnaire
All vendors must complete the vendor security questionnaire before access is granted. The questionnaire covers data handling, encryption, access controls, and breach notification practices.

### 3.2 Certifications
Vendors handling Restricted data must hold a recognised security certification such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2, or demonstrate equivalent controls through a third-party audit. Certifications are re-verified annually.

### 3.3 Access Principles
Vendor access follows least-privilege: vendors receive only the minimum access required for their scope. Access is granted with an automatic expiry of 90 days and must be renewed with business justification.

## 4. Ongoing Reviews

### 4.1 Quarterly Reviews
Vendor access and activity are reviewed quarterly by the procurement and information security teams. Access is revoked for vendors with no activity for 60 consecutive days.

### 4.2 Annual Review
An annual vendor performance review evaluates delivery, quality, and compliance. Vendors rated below the minimum threshold are placed on a corrective action plan or terminated.

## 5. Offboarding

### 5.1 Offboarding Steps
When a vendor contract ends, access is revoked within 24 hours, all company data is returned or destroyed as per the contract, and the vendor is removed from the vendor registry. The business owner confirms data return in writing.

### 5.2 Late Offboarding
If access revocation is missed, the security team is alerted automatically by the access expiry system and performs an immediate review of the vendor's account activity.
