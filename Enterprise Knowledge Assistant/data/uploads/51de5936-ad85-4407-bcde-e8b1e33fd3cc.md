# Data Privacy and Protection Policy (Confidential)

## 1. Scope and Principles

### 1.1 Scope
This policy applies to all personal data processed by Acme Corporation, including employee data, customer data, and data of third parties, whether processed in company systems or by vendors on the company's behalf.

### 1.2 Core Principles
All processing of personal data must follow these principles: lawfulness, purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation, and confidentiality and integrity. Personal data must be collected only for a specific, legitimate purpose and used only for that purpose.

## 2. Data Classification

### 2.1 Classification Tiers
Data is classified into three tiers: Public, Internal, and Restricted.
- Public: information that can be freely shared (marketing brochures).
- Internal: business information for internal use (policies, org charts).
- Restricted: personal data, financial records, and unreleased product information, which requires the highest level of protection.

### 2.2 Handling Rules
Restricted data must be stored only on approved, encrypted company systems, accessed only by authorised personnel on a need-to-know basis, and never transmitted over personal email or unencrypted channels.

## 3. Retention and Deletion

### 3.1 Retention Periods
Employee personal data is retained for 7 years after the end of employment, to satisfy statutory and audit requirements. Customer personal data is retained per the contract term plus 3 years, unless a shorter statutory period applies. Candidate data from rejected applications is deleted after 12 months.

### 3.2 Secure Deletion
Data past its retention period must be securely deleted. Physical media is destroyed through the certified disposal vendor, and digital data is purged with verification by the Information Security team.

## 4. Data Subject Rights

### 4.1 Rights
Individuals may exercise rights to access, correct, port, or request deletion of their personal data. Requests are submitted through the privacy request portal.

### 4.2 Response SLAs
Privacy requests must be acknowledged within 7 days and fully responded to within 30 days. The deadline may be extended once by 30 days for complex requests, with notice to the requester.

## 5. Breach Management

### 5.1 Internal Notification
Any suspected personal data breach must be reported to the privacy officer within 24 hours of discovery, with an initial impact assessment.

### 5.2 External Notification
Where a breach poses a risk to individuals, affected users are notified within 48 hours of the assessment, and the relevant regulator is notified within 72 hours where required by applicable law.

## 6. Vendor Obligations

### 6.1 Data Processing Agreements
Every vendor that processes personal data on behalf of Acme must sign a data processing agreement (DPA) incorporating the company's data protection obligations, before any data is shared.

### 6.2 Vendor Audits
Vendors processing Restricted data must demonstrate security controls through a recognised certification (such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2) or an equivalent third-party audit, reviewed annually by the Information Security team.
