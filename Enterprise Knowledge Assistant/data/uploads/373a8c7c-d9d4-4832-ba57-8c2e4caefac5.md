# Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) Guidelines

## 1. When an NDA Is Required

### 1.1 Mandatory Scenarios
An NDA must be signed before any of the following is shared: potential partner or vendor pricing, unreleased product roadmaps, acquisition or investment discussions, and customer data shared for proof-of-concept engagements.

### 1.2 Optional Scenarios
An NDA is optional, at the discretion of the team lead, for generic vendor capability discussions and publicly available information.

## 2. NDA Types

### 2.1 One-Way vs Two-Way
A one-way NDA protects only the disclosing party and is used when only one side shares confidential information. A two-way (mutual) NDA protects both parties and is used when both sides will exchange confidential information, such as in partnership or integration discussions.

### 2.2 Which Template to Use
Use the company's standard NDA template (version 3.2) available on the Legal portal. Non-standard templates must be reviewed by the Legal team before signing.

## 3. Key Terms in the Standard NDA

### 3.1 Definition of Confidential Information
Confidential information includes written and oral information marked or identified as confidential, plus pricing, customer lists, source code, and trade secrets regardless of marking. Information that is public, independently developed, or lawfully received from a third party is excluded.

### 3.2 Term
The confidentiality obligation under the standard NDA lasts 5 years from the date of disclosure. For trade secrets, the obligation lasts as long as the information remains a trade secret.

### 3.3 Return or Destruction
On termination of the NDA or the underlying relationship, the receiving party must return or destroy all confidential information within 30 days and certify the destruction in writing.

## 4. Signing Authority

### 4.1 Approval Limits
- Contracts with a value up to ₹10 lakh: the responsible manager may sign.
- Contracts from ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore: department head approval required.
- Contracts above ₹1 crore, or involving strategic partnerships, pricing, or customer data: Legal and the CFO must review and sign.

### 4.2 NDA Registry
Every NDA must be uploaded to the Legal portal registry within 5 working days of signing, with the counterparty name, signatory, and expiry date recorded. The Legal team sends renewal reminders 90 days before expiry.

## 5. Compliance

### 5.1 Violations
Sharing confidential information without an NDA in place is a serious policy violation subject to disciplinary action, including termination. Accidental disclosures must be reported to Legal within 24 hours.

### 5.2 Training
All employees who handle pricing or customer data must complete the annual confidentiality and IP training delivered by the Legal team.
