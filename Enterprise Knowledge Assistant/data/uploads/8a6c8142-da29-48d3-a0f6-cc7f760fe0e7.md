# FY 2026 Budget and Forecast (Confidential)

## 1. Executive Summary

### 1.1 Budget Position
The approved operating budget for FY 2026 is ₹520 crore, against a revenue target of ₹540 crore. The budget assumes 28% revenue growth over FY 2025 and a 10% operating margin.

### 1.2 Key Assumptions
The budget is built on the following assumptions: a hiring plan of 120 net new employees, a 12% cloud infrastructure cost reduction through reserved-instance purchases, and a 94% customer retention rate maintained from FY 2025.

## 2. Department Allocations

### 2.1 Allocation by Department
The FY 2026 budget is allocated as follows:
- Engineering: 34% (₹177 crore)
- Sales and Marketing: 22% (₹114 crore)
- Operations: 14% (₹73 crore)
- HR: 9% (₹47 crore)
- Finance and Legal: 5% (₹26 crore)
- Other general and administrative: 16% (₹83 crore)

### 2.2 Contingency
A contingency reserve of 3% of the total budget (₹15.6 crore) is held centrally by the CFO and released only with CFO approval.

## 3. Headcount Plan

### 3.1 Hiring by Department
The plan adds 120 net new employees: Engineering adds 55, Sales and Marketing adds 35, Operations adds 15, HR adds 8, and Finance and Legal adds 7.

### 3.2 People Cost
People cost, including salaries, variable pay, and benefits, accounts for 46% of the total budget. The merit increase of 8-10% is budgeted within this line item.

## 4. Technology Spend

### 4.1 Cloud and Infrastructure
Cloud infrastructure spend is budgeted at ₹18 crore for FY 2026, a reduction of 12% from FY 2025 achieved through reserved instances and workload consolidation. This includes all SaaS licences at 4% of total spend (₹20.8 crore) across the organisation.

### 4.2 Research and Development
Research and development spending is budgeted at 18% of revenue, consistent with the company's stated reinvestment policy.

## 5. Confidentiality and Handling

### 5.1 Distribution
This document is confidential and is to be shared only with the leadership team, the finance team, and department heads for planning purposes.

### 5.2 Revision Cycle
The budget is reviewed quarterly at the business review meeting. Actuals versus budget variance beyond 5% on any department line requires a formal re-forecast approved by the CFO.
