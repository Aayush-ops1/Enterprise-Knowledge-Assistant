# Compensation and Benefits Policy — FY 2026 (Confidential)

## 1. Salary Structure

### 1.1 Pay Components
Compensation for all employees consists of a fixed base pay, a variable pay component, and benefits. Fixed pay is paid monthly, and variable pay is paid quarterly in arrears.

### 1.2 Salary Bands by Level
The following salary bands apply for FY 2026:
- E1 (Associate): ₹4 lakh to ₹6 lakh per annum
- E2 (Senior Associate): ₹6 lakh to ₹9 lakh per annum
- E3 (Lead): ₹9 lakh to ₹14 lakh per annum
- M1 (Manager): ₹14 lakh to ₹20 lakh per annum
- M2 (Senior Manager): ₹20 lakh to ₹30 lakh per annum
- Director and above: ₹30 lakh and above, approved by the CEO

Offers are made within the band for the level and typically at the 50th percentile of the market benchmark.

## 2. Variable Pay

### 2.1 Variable Pay Percentage
The variable pay component is 10% of annual fixed pay for employee levels (E1-E3) and 15% for manager levels (M1 and above). Variable pay is paid quarterly and is linked to the company scorecard (70%) and individual performance rating (30%).

### 2.2 Payout Conditions
Variable pay is payable only if the employee is in active service on the payout date. Employees on prolonged leave of more than 60 continuous days in a quarter may have their variable pay pro-rated by the HR Business Partner.

## 3. Appraisal Cycle

### 3.1 Cycle Timing
The annual appraisal cycle runs from April to June each year, with revisions effective from 1 July. The merit increase budget is set annually by the finance team, typically in the range of 8% to 10% of the payroll for eligible employees.

### 3.2 Eligibility
Employees are eligible for a merit increase if they have completed at least 6 months of continuous service as of the revision date and have not received a performance improvement plan in the preceding 12 months.

## 4. Equity (ESOP)

### 4.1 Eligibility and Grant Size
ESOP grants are offered to employees at level E4 and above as part of the offer or annual review. Grant size is determined by level and performance rating and is approved by the compensation committee.

### 4.2 Vesting Schedule
ESOPs vest over 4 years with a 1-year cliff: 25% of the grant vests on the first anniversary of the grant date, and the remaining 75% vests in equal monthly instalments over the following 36 months.

## 5. Insurance and Other Benefits

### 5.1 Medical Insurance
All employees and their dependents (spouse, children, and parents) are covered under a group medical policy with a sum insured of ₹5 lakh per family per year. Coverage starts on the date of joining, including during probation.

### 5.2 Life and Accident Insurance
Employees are covered under a group term life policy of 3 times annual fixed pay and a group accident policy of ₹20 lakh.

### 5.3 Other Benefits
The company provides a home-office setup allowance of ₹15,000 (once per employment) and a wellness allowance of ₹10,000 per year for gym, mental health, or preventive health check-up expenses.

## 6. Confidentiality

### 6.1 Handling
This policy contains confidential compensation data. Salary bands, individual salaries, and variable pay percentages must not be shared outside the HR and Finance teams, and must not be discussed across levels.
